# Two-Sentence Horror

Entry for [make.fun — Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253)

> The babysitter texted that the baby had finally stopped crying at 3 a.m. I was still driving home, and my daughter was asleep in the back seat.

## Why this story

The horror lands in the second sentence, which quietly rewrites the first: if the daughter was in the car the whole time, then there was no baby at home — and whatever finally stopped crying at 3 a.m. was never the child the babysitter was supposed to be watching. Two concrete details, the 3 a.m. timestamp and the daughter asleep in the back seat, do all the work with no explanation, leaving the reader to imagine what the babysitter was alone with.
