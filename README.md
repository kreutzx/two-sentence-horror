# Two-Sentence Horror

Entry for [make.fun — Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253)

> Through the baby monitor, a woman sang our daughter to sleep, soft and slow. My wife gripped my arm in the doorway — we don't have a daughter.

## Why this story

The horror lands in the second sentence, which quietly rewrites the first: a stranger's voice was singing through the baby monitor to a child who does not exist. The soft, slow lullaby and the wife gripping her husband's arm in the doorway keep the scene intimate and domestic, so the final reveal — there is no daughter — does all the work with no explanation.
